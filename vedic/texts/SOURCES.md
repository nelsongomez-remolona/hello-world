Their header says they are "for personal study and research" and must not be reposted for promotion or commercial purposes. The `sa_*.txt` files come from GRETIL, which licenses for non-commercial use with attribution. Each file's own header carries its credits. All of the underlying works are ancient or medieval.

## Not included

- **An English translation of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra.** There is no pre-1931 English translation, and the standard ones (Santhanam 1984, Sharma) are under copyright. The Sanskrit original is in `sanskrit/`.
- **Phaladeepika, Jataka Parijata (full English), Uttara Kalamrita, Jaimini Sutras (astrology).** The English translations are 1930s or later and may still be under copyright.
- **Surya Siddhanta, 1935 Calcutta reprint.** It has much cleaner OCR, but it adds a 1935 introduction and the upload is marked CC BY-NC-ND.
- **Brihat Jataka (B. Suryanarain Rao, 1919) and Jataka Parijata (1922, Digital Library of India).** The scans exist, but the OCR read the English as Devanagari and is unusable.
