# Verse translations for one natal chart

These are my own English translations of the Sanskrit e-texts in `../texts/sanskrit/`. They cover only the verses for the placements below. Each entry gives the transliteration (ITRANS, as in the source file), a fairly literal translation, and a note where the reading is uncertain.

The verses come from three texts:
- **BPHS**: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
- **CC**: Chamatkara Chintamani of Narayana Bhatta
- **PD**: Phaladeepika of Mantreswara

The chart has a Capricorn ascendant with these placements:

| Planet | Sign | House | Houses ruled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Libra | 10th | 8th |
| Moon | Aries | 4th | 7th |
| Mars | Leo | 8th | 4th, 11th |
| Mercury | Libra | 10th | 6th, 9th |
| Jupiter | Sagittarius | 12th | 3rd, 12th |
| Venus | Virgo | 9th | 5th, 10th |
| Saturn | Pisces | 3rd | 1st, 2nd |
| Rahu | Virgo | 9th | — |
| Ketu | Pisces | 3rd | — |

The e-texts contain typos. Where a verse is corrupt, I give the gist and say so.

## 1. House lords: BPHS chapter 24 (`par2130.itx`)

**1st lord (Saturn) in the 3rd: 24.3**
> lagneshe sahaje jAtaH siMhatulyaparAkramI | sarvasampadyuto mAnI dvibhAryo matimAn sukhI

One born with the ascendant lord in the 3rd has courage like a lion, all kinds of wealth, self-respect, two wives, intelligence, and happiness.

**2nd lord (Saturn) in the 3rd: 24.15**
> dhaneshe sahaje jAto vikramI matimAn guNI | kAmI lobhI shubhADhye cha pApADhye devanindakaH

Valorous, intelligent and virtuous; also desirous and greedy. With benefics, [good results]. With malefics, he criticizes the gods.

*Note:* Saturn is itself a malefic, and it is joined by Ketu, so the "with malefics" clause applies. A modern reading would be a skeptic of religious authority.

**3rd lord (Jupiter) in the 12th: 24.36**
> vyayasthe sahajAdhIshe kukArye vyayakR^ijjanaH | pitA tasya bhavet krUraH strIbhirbhAgyodayastathA

He spends on wrong undertakings. His father is harsh. His fortune rises through women.

**4th lord (Mars) in the 8th: 24.44**
> sukheshe randhrabhAvasthe gR^ihAdisukhavarjitaH | pitroH sukhaM bhavedalpaM jAtaH klIbasamo bhavet

Deprived of the comforts of home and the like. Little happiness from his parents. He becomes like a *klība*.

*Note:* *klība* literally means "eunuch". Commentators also read it as "lacking drive or virility".

**5th lord (Venus) in the 9th: 24.57**
> suteshe bhAgyage putro bhapo vA tatsamo bhavet | svayaM vA granthakartA cha vikhyAtaH kuladIpakaH

His son becomes a ruler or the equal of one. Or he himself becomes an author of books, famous, the lamp of his family.

**6th lord (Mercury) in the 10th: 24.70**
> ShaShTheshe dashame bhAve mAnavaH kulavishrutaH | abhaktashcha piturvaktA videshe cha sukhI bhavet

Renowned in his family. Not devoted to his father. An eloquent speaker. **Happy in foreign lands.**

**7th lord (Moon) in the 4th: 24.76**
> dAreshe sukhabhAvasthe jAyA nAsya vashe sadA | svayaM satyapriyo dhImAn dharmAtmA dantarogayuk

His wife is not always under his control, meaning she is independent. He himself loves truth, is wise and righteous, and suffers dental trouble.

**8th lord (Sun) in the 10th: 24.94**
> randhreshe karmabhAvasthe pitR^isaukhyavivarjitaH | pishunaH karmahInashcha yadi naiva shubhekShite

Deprived of happiness from his father. A slanderer and without work, *if not aspected by benefics*.

*Note:* Mercury, a benefic, is conjunct the Sun. Many commentators count a conjunction as satisfying "aspected by a benefic", in which case the second half is cancelled.

**9th lord (Mercury) in the 10th: 24.106**
> bhAgyeshe karmabhAvasthe jAto rAjA.atha tatsamaH | mantrI senApatirvA.api guNavAn janapujitaH

He becomes a king or the equal of one, or a minister or army commander. Virtuous and honored by the people.

**10th lord (Venus) in the 9th: 24.117**
> rAjyeshe bhAgyabhe jAto rAjA rAjakulodbhavaH | tatsamo.anyakulotpanno dhanaputrAdisaMyutaH

Born into a royal family, he becomes a king. Born into another family, he becomes the equal of one, with wealth, sons and the like.

**11th lord (Mars) in the 8th: 24.128**
> lAbheshe randhrabhAvasthe hAniH kAryeShu jAyate | …

Losses occur in his undertakings.

*Note:* The rest of this verse is about lifespan and death, and is omitted deliberately.

**12th lord (Jupiter) in the 12th: 24.144**
> vyayeshe vyayabhAvasthe vyayAdhikyaM hi jAyate | na sharIrasukhaM tasya krodhI dveShaparo nR^iNAm

Excessive expenditure. No bodily comfort. Angry, and hostile toward people.

*Note:* BPHS is negative here. The later doctrine that a 12th lord in the 12th is fortunate (Vimala yoga) is not in this chapter.

## 2. The 9th–10th lord exchange: PD 6.32–34 (`phaladIpika.itx`, lines 519–528)

> anyonyaM bhavanasthayorvihagayorlagnAdirihphAntakaM bhAvAdhIshvaryoH krameNa kathitAH ShaTShaShTiyogA janaiH | trishaddainyamudIritaM vyayaripuchChidrAdinAthotthitAs tvaShTau shauryapateH khalA nigaditAH sheShA mahAkhyAH smR^itAH ‖32‖

Exchanges of sign between the lords of the 1st through 12th houses make 66 combinations.
- The 30 involving the lords of the 12th, 6th or 8th are called **Dainya**.
- The 8 involving the 3rd lord are called **Khala**.
- The rest are called **Mahā**.

> shrIkaTakShanilayaH prabhurADhyashchitravastrakanakAbharaNashcha | pArthivAptabahumAnarasamAj~no yAnavittasutavAMshcha mahAkhye ‖34‖

One born in a Mahā yoga is the abode of Lakshmi's glance, a lord, and wealthy. He has fine clothes and gold ornaments, receives honor from the ruler, and has vehicles, wealth and sons.

*Note:* The exchange of the 9th and 10th signs is a Mahā yoga. A strict reader could argue that Mercury also rules the 6th, which would make it Dainya. The usual reading goes by the two signs exchanged, which gives Mahā.

## 3. Moon in Aries: PD 9.1 (`phaladIpika.itx`, line 859)

> vR^ittekShaNo durbalajAnurugro bhIrurjale syAllaghubhuk sukAmI | saMchArashIlashchapalo.anR^itoktir vraNA~NkitA~NgaH kriyabhe prajAtaH

One born with the Moon in Aries has round eyes and weak knees. He is fierce, afraid of water, eats little, and is amorous. He is given to roaming, fickle, untruthful in speech, and has a body marked with scars.

*Note:* Brihat Jataka (Iyer, line 10325) says "strong knees". The texts disagree with each other.

## 4. Planets in houses: Chamatkara Chintamani (`chamatkarachintamani.itx`)

**Sun in the 10th: CC 1.10** *(e-text corrupt)*
> prayArto.ashubhAn yasya meShUraNe.asya shramaH sidhido rAjatulyo narasya | …

Gist: his labor yields success, and the man is like a king. The second half seems to mention suffering, and separation from loved ones. It is too garbled to translate reliably.

**Moon in the 4th: CC 2.4**
> yadA bandhuga vAndhavairatrijanmA nR^ipadvAri sarvAdhikArI sadaiva | vayasyAdime tAdR^ishaM naiva saukhyaM …

When the Moon is in the 4th, he is [honored] by his relatives and always holds full authority at the king's door, meaning high office in an institution. In the first part of life such happiness does not come.

*Note:* The last clause is uncertain.

**Mars in the 8th: CC 3.8**
> shubhAstasya kiM khecarAH kuryuranye vidhAne.apicedaShTame bhUmisUnuH | sakhA kiM na shatrUyate satkR^ito.api prayatne kR^ite bhUyate copasargaiH

If Mars is in the 8th, what can the other, benefic, planets do for him? Even a friend he has honored turns into an enemy. Even when he makes an effort, troubles arise.

**Mercury in the 10th: CC 4.10**
> mitaM saMvadenno mitaM maMlabhet … budhe karmage pUjanIyo visheShAt pituH sampado nItidaNDAdhikArAt

He speaks in measured words. With Mercury in the 10th he is especially honored. He has his father's wealth, and **authority in policy and law** (*nīti-daṇḍa*).

**Jupiter in the 12th: CC 5.12**
> yashaH kIdR^ishaM sadvyaye sAbhimAne matiH kIdR^ishI va~nchanAchet pareShAm | … trayaste bhaveyuH vyaye yasya jIvaH

What fame has the proud man whose Jupiter is in the 12th? What kind of intellect, if he deceives others? What kind of fortune, when his wealth is lost? Fame, intellect and fortune all become doubtful for him.

**Venus in the 9th: CC 6.9**
> bhR^igau trikoNe pure ke na paurAH … gR^ihe … dharmadhvajAdeH sahotthAdi saukhyaM sharIre sukhaM cha

With Venus in the trine (the 9th), which citizens of the town would not further his prosperity? At home, a banner of dharma, meaning religious or charitable works. Happiness from siblings, and bodily comfort.

**Saturn in the 3rd: CC 7.3**
> tR^itIye shanau shItalaM naiva chittaM … dR^iDhAshaH sukhI durmukhaH satkR^ito.api

With Saturn in the 3rd, the mind is never cool or at ease. Fortune is not always [unobstructed]. He is firm in purpose and happy; sharp-tongued, though honored.

**Rahu in the 9th: CC 8.9**
> manIShI kR^itaM na tyajet bandhuvargaM sadA pAlayet pUjitaH svairguNaiH | sabhAdyotako … tamaH kautukI devatIrthe dayAluH

Wise. He does not abandon what he has undertaken, and always supports his kin. He is honored for his own qualities and illuminates assemblies. Curious, devoted to temples and pilgrimage, compassionate.

**Ketu in the 3rd: CC 9.3**
> shikhI vikrame shatrunAshaM vivAdaM dhanaM bhogamaishvaryatejo.adhikaM cha | suhR^idvarganAshaM sadA bAhupIDAM bhayodvegachintA.akulatvaM vidhatte

Ketu in the 3rd destroys enemies and brings disputes. It gives wealth, enjoyment, lordship and greater radiance. It also brings loss among friends, pain in the arms, and fear, anxiety and agitation.

## 5. Navamsa (D9) and karakamsa: BPHS chapter 33 (`par3140.itx`)

The D9 for this chart:

| Position from the karakamsa | D9 sign | Planets in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1st (also the D9 ascendant) | Virgo | Jupiter, Moon, Saturn |
| 2nd | Libra | Ketu |
| 3rd | Scorpio | Mercury |
| 5th | Capricorn | Sun |
| 6th | Aquarius | Venus |
| 7th | Pisces | empty |
| 8th | Aries | Rahu |
| 9th | Taurus | Mars |
| 10th | Gemini | empty |
| 11th | Cancer | empty |
| 12th | Leo | empty |

The **karakamsa** (*svāṃśa*) is the navamsa sign of the atmakaraka, the planet at the highest degree within its sign. Here the atmakaraka is Jupiter, and the karakamsa is **Virgo**, which is also the D9 ascendant. If the Moon is the atmakaraka instead (the two are within 2′ of each other), the karakamsa is still Virgo.

Many of these verses use *yutekṣita*, "joined *or* aspected". Where a sign is empty, I apply the planets aspecting it by graha drishti, and say so.

**Karakamsa in Virgo: 33.4**
> kaNDUH sthaulya~ncha kanyAMshe tathA vahnikaNAd.hbhayam

When the atmakaraka is in a Virgo navamsa: itching (skin trouble), corpulence, and danger from sparks of fire.

**Benefics and a malefic in the karakamsa: 33.9–10**
> kArakAMshe shubhe vipra lagnAMshe cha shubhagrahe | shubhasaMvIxite jAto rAjA bhavati nishchitaH ‖ svAMshAchChubhagrahAH kendre koNe vA pApavarjitAH | dhanavidyAyuto jAto mishrairmishraphalaM vaded

If benefics occupy the karakamsa and the navamsa ascendant, and benefics aspect them, the native certainly becomes a king. If benefics are in kendras or trines from the karakamsa, free of malefics, he has wealth and learning. **With a mixture, declare mixed results.**

*Note:* Jupiter and the Moon (benefics) share the karakamsa with Saturn (a malefic), so the result is mixed.

**Full Moon in the karakamsa: 33.13**
> pUrNendrau bhogavAn vidvAn shukradR^iShTe visheShataH

With the full Moon there: an enjoyer of pleasures and learned, especially if Venus aspects it.

*Note:* The Moon is about 8° past full. Venus does not aspect it.

**Jupiter in the karakamsa: 33.16**
> sukarmA j~nAnaniShThashcha vedavit svAMshage gurau

With Jupiter there: virtuous in action, devoted to knowledge, a knower of the Veda.

**Saturn in the karakamsa: 33.17**
> shanau svAMshagate jAtaH svakulochitakarmakR^it

With Saturn there: he does the work proper to his family line.

**Moon and Jupiter in the karakamsa: 33.41–42, repeated at 33.85–88**
> svAMshe tatpa~nchame vA.api chandrejyAbhyAM cha granthakR^it | … guruNA kevalenaiva sarvavid granthakR^it tathA | vedavedAntavichchApi na vAgmI shAbdako.api san

With the Moon and Jupiter in the karakamsa or the 5th from it: **an author of books.** With Venus, somewhat less so; with Mercury, less still. With Jupiter alone: an all-knowing author and a knower of Veda and Vedanta, a master of words, **yet not an orator.**

**Saturn and the Moon in the karakamsa: 33.43–44, repeated at 33.89–90**
> sabhAjaDastu shaninA … chandreNa sAMkhyayogaj~naH sAhityaj~nashcha gAyakaH

With Saturn: **dull or tongue-tied in assemblies.** With the Moon: a knower of Sankhya and Yoga, versed in literature, a singer.

**Ketu in the 2nd from the karakamsa: 33.31, 33.93**
> ketau tatpratibandhaH syAt … ketau svAMshAd.hdvitIye vA tR^itIye stabdhavAg bhavet | pApadR^iShTe visheSheNa mAnavo vaktumaxamaH

Ketu there checks the tendency described in the verse before, which is about adultery. With Ketu in the 2nd or 3rd from the karakamsa, the native's **speech is stiff**. If malefics aspect it, he is especially unable to speak.

*Note:* No malefic aspects this Ketu.

**A benefic in the 3rd or 6th from the karakamsa: 33.32, 33.46**
> tasmin shubhagrahe jAtaH kAtaro … ShaShThagate pApe karShako … shubhagrahe.alasashcheti tR^itIye.api

With a benefic in the 3rd from the karakamsa, the native is timid. With a malefic in the 6th he is a cultivator; with a benefic there, **lazy**. The same applies to the 3rd.

*Note:* Mercury is in the 3rd and Venus in the 6th, both benefics.

**Malefics in the trines from the karakamsa: 33.75–76**
> kArakAMshAt trikoNasthe pApakhetadvaye dvija | mAnavo mantratantraj~no jAyate … shubhairnirIxite tasmin naro.anugrAhako bhavet

With two malefics in the trines from the karakamsa: **a knower of mantra and tantra.** If malefics aspect them, he uses that knowledge to restrain or harm. If benefics aspect them, he uses it to bless and help.

*Note:* The Sun is in the 5th and Mars in the 9th from the karakamsa. Jupiter aspects both, by its 5th- and 9th-house aspects from Virgo, so the "help" reading applies.

**Sun in the 5th from the karakamsa: 33.39, 33.84**
> ravau khaDgadharo jAtaH

With the Sun there: a sword-bearer.

**The 7th from the karakamsa (empty; aspected by the Moon, Jupiter and Saturn): 33.47–48**
> dyUne chandraguru yasya bhAryA tasyAtisundarI | … shanau chApi vayo.adhikA

With the Moon and Jupiter in the 7th: **a very beautiful wife.** With Saturn: **a wife older in age.**

*Note:* These verses name planets *in* the 7th, so applying them by aspect is an extension.

**The 9th from the karakamsa (Mars in it; Jupiter aspects it): 33.50–51**
> kArakAMshAchcha navame shubhagrahayutexite | satyavAdI gurau bhaktaH svadharmanirato naraH ‖ … pApagrahayutexite | svadharmanirato bAlye mithyAvAdI cha vArdhake

With benefics joining or aspecting it: truthful, devoted to his teacher, and steady in his own dharma. With malefics: steady in his own dharma in youth, but **false in speech in old age.**

*Note:* Both conditions are present.

**The 10th from the karakamsa (empty; Saturn aspects it): 33.58**
> dashame kArakAMshAchcha pApakheTayutexite | vyApAre jAyate hAniH pitR^isaukhyena varjitaH

With a malefic joining or aspecting the 10th from the karakamsa: **losses in trade, and no happiness from the father.**

**The 11th from the karakamsa (empty; the Sun aspects it): 33.62**
> ekAdashe sapApe tu kumArgAllAbakR^innaraH | vikhyAto vikramI chaiva

With a malefic there: he gains by an irregular path, and is famous and valorous.

**The 12th from the karakamsa (empty; Venus and Mars aspect it): 33.63**
> kArakAMshAd vyayasthAne sad.hgrahe sad.hvyayo bhavet | asad.hvyayo.ashubhe j~neyo grahAbhAve cha satphalam

With a benefic there: spending on good things. With a malefic: spending on bad things. With no planet there: a good result.

*Note:* The sign is empty, but one benefic and one malefic aspect it.

*Omitted:* 33.49, which judges lifespan from the 8th from the karakamsa.
