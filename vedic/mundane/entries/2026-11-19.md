## Classical checks (Brihat Samhita, Iyer 1884, ch. 17 and the chapter on planetary motions)

**Planetary war.** Mars and Jupiter came within about 1° around 15–18 Nov. By BS 17.10 the brighter planet wins, and Jupiter (about −2.5 magnitude) is far brighter than Mars (about +0.3; my estimate), so Mars is defeated. BS 17.13: "If Mars should suffer defeat in his conjunction with Jupiter, the Balhikas, travellers and persons that live by fire will suffer." The Balhikas are the people of Balkh, in present-day northern Afghanistan. Afghanistan news not yet checked. "Travellers" loosely fits the Senegal shipwreck.

**Checked and found not to apply:**
- "Sun at the end of Taurus/Gemini/Scorpio/Leo with Jupiter and Mars in conjunction with it" (famine). The Sun is at the end of Scorpio, but it squares Mars and Jupiter rather than conjoining them.
- "Saturn, Mars and Jupiter within sight of each other" (hunger and weapons). Sidereally only partial: Mars aspects Saturn, but not the reverse. It applies tropically, by trine.

**Pending:** "If the course of Mars and Saturn, through the signs of Cancer, Leo and Pisces should be retrograde… there would be much suffering on Earth." Saturn has been retrograde in sidereal Pisces in 2026, and Mars is retrograde in sidereal Leo and then Cancer in early 2027. See forecast M5.
