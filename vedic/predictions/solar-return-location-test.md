# Solar return location test: where you are vs birthplace

**Question:** should an annual chart be cast for where you are on the birthday (the location chart), or always for the birthplace (Manila)? Cities across the world see the same planets in the same signs at the moment of return. Only the house placements change.

**Years available**

|---|---|---|
| Oct 2023 | San Francisco (assumed) | No. Every house topic was eventful that year, so every chart scores the same. |
| **Oct 2024** | **Honolulu** | Yes: Honolulu vs SF (home) vs Manila (birthplace) |
| Oct 2025 | San Francisco | Yes: SF vs Manila |
| **Oct 2026** | **Girona** | Prospective: predictions below, written before the year happened |

**Correction to `annual-blind-test-2023-2026.md`:** its Year B (Oct 2024) charts were cast for San Francisco. You were in Honolulu, so those were the wrong charts if location matters. The SF tropical chart still scored 6/7 on the discriminating questions. That shows how flexible judgement-based reading is, and it's why this test uses a mechanical rule.

## Method (mechanical, retrospective)

1. Truth table: from the answers already given, each of the 12 houses is marked eventful (1) or quiet (0) for the year.
   - 2024: quiet = 7th (no disputes or partner), 8th (no others'-money paperwork), 9th (no travel abroad).
   - 2025: quiet = 11th (withdrew from friends).
2. Prediction: a house is eventful if it contains any of these bodies: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, North Node, and (tropical only) Uranus, Neptune, Pluto.
3. Score: the number of the 12 houses that match. The p-value is the share of all ways of arranging the same number of empty houses that score at least as well.

**Caveat:** the truth tables were written after the answers were known, by the same person who reads the charts. The mechanical rule limits that bias but doesn't remove it.
